## Users

- people who already know a board can look even until the leftover stripe shows
- anyone who refuses to treat a curl wave, a ray spoke, a year-band, a stain wash, a sap edge-band, a quilt blister, an eye dimple, a burl swirl, a heartwood core, a knot, a packed sky, or a clock as this leftover
- desks that want ribbon as a sketch, not a mill sign-off
- teams that want a no-backend, local-only pass — not Curl, not Ray, not Stain, not a paste well

## Workflow

1. Load the seed: 34 mm of leftover ribbon — already showing, not an even-only face
2. Read the scene: one board in face, a leftover whose leftover is the ribbon, leftover labelled as a sketch
3. Move ribbon (or use the arrow keys on the focused slider); leftover names showing or even
4. Drop the leftover toward 0 mm and the board looks even / no leftover ribbon; raise it and more / wider leftover stripe-bands still sit in the face
5. Reset restores the seeded leftover showing

## Data model

One board in face with leftover ribbon as leftover stripe that still sits in the figure:

- `ribbon` — millimetres of leftover stripe showing (default 34)

Derived picture:

- leftover labelled as a sketch (even / showing), not a mill sign-off
- leftover stripe labelled as a sketch
- even / no leftover ribbon when ribbon ≤ 4 mm (the board looks even throughout)

Nothing is persisted. Refresh restores the seeded scene.
