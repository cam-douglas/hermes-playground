local-first retail replenishment cockpit
Reorder Radar

Know what to buy before the shelf goes empty.

A lightweight inventory and replenishment cockpit for pop-up shops, kiosks, and small retailers. Instead of a heavy ERP, it keeps the list small, the reorder queue clear, and the decision loop visible.

Open dashboard Add a SKU Back to catalogue

Problem

Teams know what sold yesterday, but not what should be reordered today or which items are about to miss lead time.

Users

Pop-up operators, boutique managers, kiosk staff, and anyone who needs a reorder list without adopting a full POS stack.

Workflow

Receive stock, record sales, watch coverage days fall, then buy the smallest safe quantity before the next shift.

Product notes

Architecture: static HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, local persistence. Data model: stock, reorder point, demand velocity, lead time, and unit cost. Deployment: ship to Vercel as a static product in its own folder.
