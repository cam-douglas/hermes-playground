Research signal

The GitHub feed is agent harnesses — skill packs, token compression, graphs. This catalogue is the other desk: human-ops instruments that sit beside that feed. Local-first, no backend, no new cron.

Problem

A cut looks clean until you measure the gap. The missing local product is Kerf: leftover removed by the blade. Not Offcut (remaining stock length). Not Grain (cut angle). Not Fold Sheet (creases). Not a paste well. Not a clock.

Prototype scope

One SVG scene: a stock plank with a single cut through it. Seed already a visible bite — leftover millimetres the blade ate, sitting as a gap — so the removed leftover is obvious. Move kerf width or stock thickness; the gap redraws. Reset restores the seed. Local-only. ?embed=1 hides the catalogue chrome so the living well can iframe it. Desk keys still walk the cards and seed on featured Kerf.

Catalogue desk

Filter the thirty-two live products by name, blurb, slug, tag, or ship day. Search, tag, and day compose with AND, persist as ?q=, ?tag=, and ?day=, and copy as a desk link. Compact archive is ?view=compact. A compare well persists as ?compare= — leftover overlap, not a second filter; it does not hide cards. The featured card is a living well: an iframe of the featured href plus ?embed=1, not a copy of the SVG. Press / to focus search; Escape clears filters. Press j / k or the arrows to walk the visible cards; Enter opens the selected product. Selection is session-only and skips filtered-out cards. The ship tape and the desk sky follow that same AND filter — matching ticks and stars stay bright; the rest dim. Hours still come from one list: the run rail, the hours index, and the reader all read runs/hours.json. Products still come from one list: cards, tape, sky, and the compare well all read catalog.json. Product URLs for the earlier thirty-one are unchanged.
