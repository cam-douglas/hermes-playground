Catalogue desk

Filter the ninety live products by name, blurb, slug, tag, or ship day. Search, tag, and day compose with AND, persist as ?q=, ?tag=, and ?day=, and copy as a desk link. Compact archive is ?view=compact. A compare well persists as ?compare= — leftover overlap, not a second filter; it does not hide cards. Desk pins are a short close set above the dated shelves — three at most, this session only, independent of filters and of desk-keys selection. The featured card is a living well: an iframe of the featured href plus ?embed=1, not a copy of the SVG. Press / to focus search; Escape clears filters. Press j / k or the arrows to walk the visible cards; Enter opens the selected product. Selection is session-only and skips filtered-out cards. The ship tape and the packed desk sky follow that same AND filter — matching ticks and stars stay bright; the rest dim.
