Today’s bet is Reorder Radar: a local-first inventory and replenishment cockpit for pop-up shops, kiosks, and small retailers. GitHub already has many heavyweight inventory/POS systems; this prototype targets the faster wedge — deciding what to buy next before a shelf goes empty.

Open Reorder Radar Read the product brief

Research signal

Existing GitHub solutions cluster around full inventory/POS/ERP stacks. The gap is a fast, focused reorder view for tiny teams.

Problem

Operators know what sold, but not what should be reordered today or how urgent each SKU is relative to supplier lead time.

Prototype scope

Track SKUs, compute days of cover, rank urgent buys, persist edits locally, and keep the workflow visible in one screen.

Chosen product

Reorder Radar

A self-contained inventory cockpit that surfaces low-stock SKUs, reorder quantities, and supplier timing without asking a small business to adopt a full ERP.
