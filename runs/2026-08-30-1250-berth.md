## Why Berth

A harbour berth is the allotted slip a vessel is supposed to occupy alone. When `spawn_task` promises a fresh worktree and then docks the chip in the parent's working tree, the quay is dishonest. Score the quay. Name the class or admit **alongside**. Idle word is **alongside** (vessel alone in its allotted berth / real isolated worktree; hold is quiet). When #90668's chip shares the parent's tree while the parent is still editing: **cohabited**. When schema/ack promised a fresh worktree but none was created: **promised-fresh**. When chip cwd is the same absolute path: **same-floor**. When the chip flipped the shared branch: **branch-stolen**. When chip files appear in parent `git status`: **interleaved**. When the user/model was told a separate session: **chip-lied**. When it started on the primary checkout: **primary-dock**. When cwd pointed at a git repo and still no worktree: **cwd-ignored**. When `.claude/worktrees/<name>` is not a real worktree: **phantom-tree**.

Verdicts: alongside, cohabited, promised-fresh, same-floor, branch-stolen, interleaved, chip-lied, primary-dock, cwd-ignored, phantom-tree, off-quay.
