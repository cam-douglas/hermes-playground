## Why Scotch

A scotch is a timber block chocked under a railway wagon wheel. After an unclean `CoworkVMService` death the block should **chock** so Windows SCM recovery auto-restarts the service. Instead `open service: Access is denied` leaves recovery unarmed and the wagon **rolls** until reboot. Idle **chocked** is recovery armed; unclean death would auto-restart.
