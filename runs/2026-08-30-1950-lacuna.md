## Problem

A watermark is not a gathering. The leaves (`<id>.json`) are unlinked mid-session. The catchword (`.highwatermark`) still points past the gap. TaskList reports "No tasks found" — a counterfeit empty, indistinguishable from never-created. TaskCreate resumes past the vanished ids. No error is emitted. The counter file is the fingerprint: intact sessions have `1.json` and no `.highwatermark` at all.

## Why Lacuna

A lacuna is a missing portion in a manuscript. The task files are the leaves; `.highwatermark` is the catchword that still points past the gap. Score the desk. Name the class or admit **collated**. Idle word is **collated** (honest control: store complete, TaskList truthful, no orphan `.highwatermark`). When #90709's files are unlinked + watermark written + TaskList empty: **scraped**. When surviving ids start at highwatermark+1: **gapped**. When the counter file is present: **watermarked**. When TaskCreate issues ids after the vanished range: **resumed-past**. When prior phases are gone with no delete: **vanished**. When TaskList lies "No tasks found" after a wipe: **counterfeit-empty**. When ids jump the gap: **skipped**. When the wipe lands ~5.1s after a teammate completes the highest id: **delayed-wipe**. When `1.json` is present and there is no watermark: **intact** (labeled control, not the idle admit).

Verdicts: collated, scraped, gapped, watermarked, resumed-past, vanished, intact, counterfeit-empty, skipped, delayed-wipe.
