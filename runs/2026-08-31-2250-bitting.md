## Problem

CONCURRENT SESSIONS SHARE ONE OAUTH CREDENTIAL. MOST-RECENT MINT WINS. PROTOCOL-NEGOTIATION PROBE HANGS AND IS MISLABELED AS CONNECT_TIMEOUT. SLACK IS MISSING FROM THE NEEDS-AUTH CACHE.

## Why Bitting

A key's bitting is the cut pattern that must match the tumblers. Only the most recently cut key (most recently minted OAuth token) turns the lock. Sibling sessions still hold the previous cut — they bind in the wards and hang, reported as a network timeout instead of a wrong key. Idle **seated** is a key that seats and initialize answers. Seeded **bound** is the #90970 probe hang. A sibling key with yesterday's cut is not a hold. Score the tumblers or admit **seated**.
