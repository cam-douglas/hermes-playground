## Problem

STEALTH CONTINUITY LIE. The window and session cards are restored; the processes are not. The user has no signal that the drawers left the carcase.

## Why Carcase

A cabinetmaker's carcase is the structural box of a cabinet — the shell that drawers fit into. After stealth-relaunch the box is back on the bench (window restored, 50 nav entries, cards still listed) and every drawer is empty (CLI children killed). A gutted carcase is not a hold. Score the drawers. Name the class or admit **fitted**. Idle word is **fitted**. When stealth relaunch restored chrome and the processes are gone: **gutted**. When the box is present and the interiors are empty: **hollow**. When `[stealth-relaunch]` / `local-session-stop-all` killed live PTYs: **stealth-killed**. When the window and 50 nav entries came back and zero sessions restarted: **chrome-only**. When the restart fired without user action: **unconsented**. When nine sessions died in one second: **emptied**. When the cards look healthy and send fails `computer_unreachable`: **dummy**. When a tally of live local processes sits behind the cards: **occupied**. When the nav-restore marker loaded 50 entries and 0 sessions: **restored-nav**.

Verdicts: fitted, gutted, hollow, stealth-killed, chrome-only, unconsented, emptied, dummy, occupied, restored-nav.
