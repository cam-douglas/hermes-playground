## Problem

## Why Gaff

A vaudeville gaff is the brass shepherd's crook that yanks an act from the wings. When the crook is seen and billed as a crook, the house is honest. When the playbill still says FULL SHOW / COMPLETE after the crook, that is the lie. Score the crook. Name the class or admit **yanked**. Idle word is **yanked** (the crook was seen; the kill was reported as a kill; playbill is not stamped COMPLETE). Honest-complete (no kill and DONE present) is also **yanked**. When #90616's timeout killed the loop and the notification said completed exit 0: **billed**. When output stopped mid-stream with no DONE/TOTAL: **truncated**. When only a prefix of N iterations completed: **midloop**. When SIGKILL hit and traps never fired: **sigkilled**. When the whole process group was reaped and the after-marker was missing: **group-reaped**. When the kill was at a turn boundary and status mismatched the process: **turn-killed**. When the output file was 0 bytes and still completed exit 0: **empty-ok**. When the model told the user success and remaining units never ran: **hours-lost**.

Verdicts: yanked, billed, truncated, midloop, sigkilled, group-reaped, turn-killed, empty-ok, hours-lost.

The stage scores one new fact: HARNESS KILLS A BACKGROUNDED BASH COMMAND, THEN REPORTS COMPLETED EXIT 0. Did the crook get billed as a crook?
