## Problem

A working-size coil is not a hold. 261 worktrees. 524 worktree-admin denies. 130.7KB single arg. E2BIG. Even `sleep 5` fails.

## Why Clew

A clew is the corner of a sail the sheet is reeved through. Score the loft. Name the class or admit **rove**. Idle word is **rove** (sheet reeved; clew a working size; bash can spawn). When the #90569 coil is already past the load line: **fouled**. When the deny list grew two entries per worktree without bound: **overcoiled**. When every Bash spawn fails with E2BIG: **choked**. When the pairing is ~2 denies per worktree: **twinned**. When deny count is unbounded vs a fixed baseline: **swollen**. When a single `/bin/bash -c` argument exceeds 128KB: **jammed**. When worktrees are removed and the profile is rebuilt: **pruned**. When a cached profile still fouls after prune: **cached**. When recursive deny globs expand per-file: **globbed**.

Verdicts: rove, fouled, overcoiled, choked, twinned, swollen, jammed, pruned, cached, globbed.

The loft scores one new fact: DENY LIST GROWS TWO ENTRIES PER WORKTREE → SINGLE BWRAP ARGV CROSSES MAX_ARG_STRLEN → EVERY BASH SPAWN DIES WITH E2BIG. Did the sheet still pay?
