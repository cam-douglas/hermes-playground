## Problem

UserPromptSubmit (and sibling hook events) are the user's primary steering channel. Two silent failure modes: (A) mid-turn / queued messages never spawn the hook process; (B) the hook runs and returns additionalContext, but it never reaches the model transcript. Zero hook-execution telemetry, so users cannot self-diagnose. A fired hook is not a seated injection.

## Why Tappet

Tappet is the follower that a cam lobe must strike for the valve to seat. The hook process is the lobe. additionalContext in the transcript is the seated valve. Score the train. Name the class or admit **seated**. Idle word is **seated** (injection landed in the transcript; valve seated). When the mid-turn send never spawns: **missed**.

Verdicts: seated, missed, slipped, folded, mute, oversize, misfiled, inert, blind, wave.
