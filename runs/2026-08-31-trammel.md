Facts from the issue only:

- Two Claude Code webview panels visible at once in two different editor groups. Window loses then regains OS focus. The two panels enter a rapid mutual focus-steal loop. Input flickers; typing is impossible until a click breaks it.
- Each Claude panel registers a window-level `focus` listener that restores focus to its composer. The only guard is `document.activeElement` (`!activeElement || activeElement === document.body`).
- Each webview is its own iframe with its own `document`. Focus is exclusive across the window, but `activeElement` is per-document, so the unfocused panel's `activeElement` is `body` and the guard passes.
- `safeFocus` is gated on `isVisible` from `WebviewPanel.visible`. With split editor groups the active tab of each group reports `visible === true`. Both panels pass.
- The extension tracks `panel.active` in `onDidChangeViewState` (`if (e.active) this.setActivePanel(e)`), but `visibility_changed` sent to the webview carries only `isVisible`.
- `setTimeout(..., 0)` in both handlers keeps the two cycles offset, sustaining the loop.
- `document.hasFocus()` is not a sufficient gate — it is true in every iframe of the focused window.
- Suggested fix: include `panel.active` in `visibility_changed` as `isActive` and gate both window-`focus` handlers on it.
- Workaround: keep both Claude tabs in the same editor group so only one is visible. A non-Claude tab in the other group has the same effect. #71809 also notes one-session-per-window and `claudeCode.useTerminal: true`.
- Previously reported: #71809 (closed stale; refile note), #79770 Bug 3. Still present in 2.1.251. Not known to have ever worked.

## Why Trammel

A hunting trammel is an Archimedean ellipsograph: two perpendicular grooves, two sliding pins, a beam, a tracing point. If the pins hunt the grooves instead of riding them, the ellipse is a scribble. Two visible Claude composers on a split sash are those pins. Idle **traced** is a true ellipse (only the active pane restores). Seeded **hunting** is the #90936 steal loop. A hunting trammel is not a hold. Score the grooves or admit **traced**.
