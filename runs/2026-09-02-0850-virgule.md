## Why Virgule

A virgule is the typographic slash. A compositor seats it in the composing stick at a word boundary so the next sort can be picked. The slash/skills rail should open wherever `/` is typed at a word boundary. Instead the trigger is bound to message index 0 — a virgule that only strikes at the left of the stick.
