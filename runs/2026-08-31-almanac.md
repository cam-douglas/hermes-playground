## Why Almanac

An almanac is a printed annual of feast days. The clerk's manuscript ledger is the day-book of what is actually scheduled. The printed page still gazettes two one-shot feasts as recurring Loops a year out. The ledger is empty. A lookup-by-id stamp comes back not-found. The panel is not holding a live job; it is holding a ghost annual. Score the feast page. Name the class or admit **dated**. Idle word is **dated** (honest control: fired+gone; panel is a ghost annual, not a live Loop). When #90804's panel still shows Loop / Next 364d while CronList is empty and CronDelete is not-found: **looped**. When month/day with no year is projected as next year: **annual**. When the one-shot already ran: **fired**. When CronList is blank: **emptied**. When lookup-by-id misses: **not-found**. When Next is 364d 23h: **next-364d**. When Ends is 3d: **ends-3d**. When recurring:false is labeled Loop: **one-shot-lie**. When the feast is still printed after the clerk struck the manuscript: **gazetted**.

Verdicts: dated, looped, annual, fired, emptied, not-found, next-364d, ends-3d, one-shot-lie, gazetted.
