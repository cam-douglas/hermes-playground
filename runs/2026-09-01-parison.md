## Why Parison

A parison is the intermediate glass bubble on the blowpipe. The boys (subagents) already blew the piece (files exist) but the parison hangs in the glory hole and never reaches the punty / gaffer (parent ledger). Idle **marvered** is parent received results, stream live, cost reported, ledger settled. Seeded **hung** is the #91037 wedge. A hung parison is not a hold. Score the gather or admit **marvered**.
