## Why Chad

A chad is the scrap punched from a ballot card. When it hangs, the machine still reads a vote the elector did not cast. Score the ballot. Name the class or admit **spoilt**. Idle word is **spoilt** (spoilt ballot, nothing scored). When the tool reports a selection the user denies: **punched**.

Verdicts: spoilt, punched, blank, carried, miscast, phantom, rubbered, forced, defaulted, clear.
