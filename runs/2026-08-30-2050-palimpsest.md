## Why Palimpsest

A palimpsest is a scraped parchment — the overtext sits on a page whose undertext was erased. The model's timeout is the undertext. The hook's command rewrite is the overtext. Score the undertext. Name the class or admit **underwrit**. Idle word is **underwrit** (honest control: hook returns merged/full tool_input, siblings preserved, timeout honored). When #90725's command-only `updatedInput` whole-replaces the input: **scraped**. When siblings are absent after rewrite: **sibling-lost**. When the command dies at the 2m default: **timeout-killed**. When `run_in_background` is silently lost: **bg-dropped**. When the hook authored only `{command}`: **partial-write**. When the transcript still shows timeout: **transcript-lies**. When #90726's PIPESTATUS rewrite strips auto-background: **post-rewrite-cliff**. When a full copy+override honors timeout: **merged-keeps**.

Verdicts: underwrit, scraped, sibling-lost, timeout-killed, bg-dropped, partial-write, transcript-lies, post-rewrite-cliff, merged-keeps.
