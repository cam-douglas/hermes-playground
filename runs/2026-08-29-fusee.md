## Problem

A written cron is not a hold. The scheduler fires before the configured fireAt / cron slot. Authors now hand-write wall-clock guards into every task prompt (41+) because nothing in the platform prevents early dispatch.

## Why Fusee

A fusee is the conical pulley that equalizes mainspring force so a clock does not race. When the scheduler fires early, the spring has raced — the fusee desk catches it. Score the dial. Name the class or admit **wound**. Idle word is **wound** (spring regulated; fire time honored). When the ~95-day DST fleet rewrite would have applied months early: **early**.

Verdicts: wound, early, sprung, raced, ahead, jumped, premature, voided, held, true.
