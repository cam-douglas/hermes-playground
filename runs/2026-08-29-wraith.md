## Problem

A grant that is still ON is not a hold. The updater unlinks the binary under a live agent session. The process becomes a ghost of a deleted image. Grants stay lit. Spawns and remote-control still look healthy. Reads and child execs silently die. Full Disk Access cannot help. The only reliable fix is restart.

## Why Wraith

A wraith is a process that still “lives” after its body — the on-disk binary — was deleted. Score the image. Name the class or admit **unlinked**. Idle word is **unlinked** (image seated, or the desk is idle). When the updater removes the running version from disk while the session is live: **pruned**.

Verdicts: unlinked, pruned, ghosted, voided, orphaned, severed, stale, resurfaced, ejected, held.
