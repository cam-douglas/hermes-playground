## Thesis

A vouch can still be “live” while the human who signed it has not opened the pack in weeks. Calendar expiry is the wrong signal. Trust should decay by last re-read. Ship a half-life board: live attestations, last-opened timestamps, a decay curve, and a red “stale but still signed” rail.

This is not Vouch Slip. Vouch Slip answers “who vouches and when does the slip expire.” Reread Clock answers “has that person actually looked at the pack since they signed.”

## Inspiration

- Last hour’s reserve wedge: a *decision half-life* clock for attestations that are still live but nobody has re-read
