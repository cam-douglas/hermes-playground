## Thesis

A timestamp is one string. Two people hear two times. Paste once, see two analog faces. No send queue. No quiet-hours rail.

This is not Quiet Landing (queued sends). This is not Pager Face (on-call rotation). This is not Unseen Ink (glyphs). One paste, two clocks.
