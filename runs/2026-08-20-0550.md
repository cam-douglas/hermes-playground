## Thesis

On-call is a wiki list. At 3am you need a clock: whose night is it, when does it hand off, is the backup actually awake. Ship a 24-hour pager face, not a roster table.

This is not Quiet Landing (outbound send courtesy). This is not Still Inside (leftover access). Rotation geometry, not a row board.
