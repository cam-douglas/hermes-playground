## Problem

SILENT PREFIX RECUT. The trim bin is the conversation prefix. Pulling an early image-frame from the reel silently recuts the print, so every later impression is a full reprint at cache-creation rates.

## Why Moviola

A 1924 Moviola is a film-editing machine whose operator pulls frames from a reel into a hanging trim bin. The conversation prefix is that reel. Once ~40 page-sized frames accumulate, the earliest ones are pulled. The splice is invisible — no error, no warning — until the footage counters show `cache_read` sitting on the system+tools floor and `cache_creation` reprinting 600–740k tokens. A recut print is not a hold. Score the splice. Name the class or admit **latched**. Idle word is **latched**. When earliest images dropped, prefix mutated, and the print is fully re-cached: **recut**. When the prefix was rewritten but burn numbers are not yet on the counter: **mutated**. When earliest frames sit in the trim bin: **evicted**. When 600–740k was rewritten as cache-creation: **recached**. When the 5.7× burn ate a 5-hour allowance in 31 minutes: **burned**. When `cache_read` sits at ~26,314: **collapsed**. When there is no error, no warning, and `context_management` is null: **silent**. When promptTotal moves +113–149 after collapse: **one-in-one-out**. When cache breakpoints after system/tools were invalidated: **prefix-rewritten**.

Verdicts: latched, recut, mutated, evicted, recached, burned, collapsed, silent, one-in-one-out, prefix-rewritten.
