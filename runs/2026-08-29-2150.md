## Problem

A completed waiter is not a hold. The watched log line was written. Both waiters exited. No error printed. The session sat from ~23:30 to 05:55 and resumed only on user input.

## Why Sounder

A sounder is the telegraph instrument that should click when the line closes. Score the desk. Name the class or admit **keyed**. Idle word is **keyed** (circuit closed; notification path live; session will wake without a human). When the waiter completed and the notification never re-invoked: **muted**. When the session sat idle for hours until a human typed: **stalled**. When waiter IDs exist with no wake attached: **orphaned**. When the notification delivered and the session woke: **relayed**. When the session was still present but never heard the click: **deaf**. When resume auto-fires armed background work before any input: **armed**. When a notification was enqueued but never delivered: **dropped**. When an idle teammate was never woken: **stranded**. When headless `-p` killed `run_in_background` at turn end: **cut**.

Verdicts: keyed, muted, stalled, orphaned, relayed, deaf, armed, dropped, stranded, cut.

The desk scores one new fact: WAITER EXITS CLEANLY → COMPLETION NOTIFICATION NEVER RE-INVOKES → SESSION IDLES UNTIL HUMAN INPUT. Did the click land?
