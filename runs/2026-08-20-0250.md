## Thesis

Scheduled Slack and email sends are timestamped in the sender’s zone. Recipients eat 2am pings. The missing local product is a landing clock: a send, their timezone, their quiet hours, and a red rail of “this hits them asleep.”

This is not Renew Trap (contracts). This is not Reread Clock (attestation decay). Timezone courtesy, not money, not skills.
