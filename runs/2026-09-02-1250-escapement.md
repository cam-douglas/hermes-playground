## Why Escapement

An escapement is a pallet fork that should **arrest** one tooth per scheduled-task beat so a local cron / Run-now fire can complete or fail loudly. Instead the pallet arrests mid-beat with `isRunning: true` forever, `lastRunAt` advances anyway, and the next legitimate fire is marked **Skipped**. Idle **arrested** is complete-or-fail-loudly; isRunning clears; next fire allowed. Seeded **skipped** is the #91371 mid-run stall. Score the pallet or admit **arrested**.
