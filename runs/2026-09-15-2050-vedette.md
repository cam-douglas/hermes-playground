When the model omits `run_in_background`, CLI still backgrounds (`is_backgrounded: true`, tool_result placeholder "Async agent launched successfully"). Main turn ends. Offline control with two long-sleep backgrounded subagents exited ~651s after previous turn while subagents still alive. Explicit `run_in_background: false` keeps foreground and returns results. Workaround: `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_BACKGROUND_TASKS=1`.
