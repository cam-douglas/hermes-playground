## Problem

WRITE TOOL EMITS `.ps1` AS UTF-8 WITHOUT BOM. PowerShell 5.1 assays ANSI. An em dash becomes a quotation mark. Scheduled tasks report success and never run.

## Why Puncheon

A puncheon is the punch that strikes the hallmark. Without a trustworthy mark (the UTF-8 BOM), the reader assays the metal as the shop's ANSI and the em-dash strike comes out a quote. Idle **hallmarked** is a sheet that still parses. Seeded **misstruck** is the #90962 BOM-less em-dash wipe. An unstruck mark is not a hold. Score the gold or admit **hallmarked**.
