## Why Alidade

An alidade is the sighting vane on a plane-table. The viewer stands at one peg and sights another station. If that station has no plate, the peg is unmarked — every tool call may already be running on the foreign desk. Idle **stationed** is viewer host matches tool host, plate shown, writes land here. Seeded **displaced** is the #91055 foreign-host attach. A displaced sight is not a hold. Score the peg or admit **stationed**.
