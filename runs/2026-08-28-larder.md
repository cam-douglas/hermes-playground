## Problem

A workspace plugin store reports successful syncs on its timer while downloading nothing. `manifest.json` `lastUpdated` advances; plugin folders stand still; claude.ai Plugins page and auto-sync indicators stay green; `main.log` is silent. Sessions (Desktop / Cowork) load weeks-stale plugin content with no fault. A plugin off/on toggle is a one-shot unstick, then the store re-freezes. Fault is per-store, not machine-wide. A healthy lastUpdated is not a hold. Content must arrive on the shelf.

## Why Larder

A larder keeps a content clock: what is actually on the shelf, not the stamp on the door. The store already records a sync stamp; it does not surface when content last arrived. Those two values diverge in this fault, and the gap is the entire signal. Score the stillroom. Name the class or admit **stocked**. Idle word is **stocked** (content arrived on the shelf; hold is current). When the stamp moves and the folders do not: **stamped**.

Verdicts: stocked, stamped, frozen, greened, toggled, drifted, lagged, aisled, aged, served.
