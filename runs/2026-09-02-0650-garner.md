## Why Garner

A garner is a grain store / granary loft. Archive should empty the bin (air the loft). Instead Desktop releases the filled bin into a silent stock that never expires — grain (build artifacts) sits forever with no TTL and no loft ledger. Idle **aired** is archive removes the worktree; bin empty; disk reclaimed. Seeded **stocked** is the #91246 pool retain. Score the loft or admit **aired**.
