Thesis

When the Bash tool's bwrap sandbox is active, mount setup binds the entire /home directory rather than scoping to $HOME. Bind does not change ownership; /home is typically root:root 755. Writes that land on bare /home/.mcp.json fail with Permission denied even when $HOME is correctly /home/<user>. Observed under CLAUDE_CODE_SUBPROCESS_ENV_SCRUB=1 and --safe-mode.
