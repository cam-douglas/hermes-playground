Why Larum is not a clone

Not Oubliette/#92095. Oubliette voids or forgets child notices against a parent that never receives the queue — the notice never lands until an unrelated wake. Larum's notice is fully written into conversation history. The missing piece is specifically the model invocation / turn schedule after that append.

Not Tocsin / Alarum / Knell / Hangfire / Heliostat / Nixie. Those are different wake, queue, and delivery paradigms. Cite only as cousins. Alarum is the opposite polarity: a post-goodbye kill notification starts a turn after the session has already ended. Hangfire demotes a queued /compact. Heliostat waits on DECSET 2031. Nixie reports queued then settles undelivered. #88742 is related and different: enqueue succeeds but the wake effect is missed, so the item may sit undelivered. Here the item is already in the ledger.
