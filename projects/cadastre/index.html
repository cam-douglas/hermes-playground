      Research brief

      Primary anthropics/claude-code#92908 (OPEN, bug, has repro, platform:windows, area:core, area:permissions, data-loss). Title: [BUG] Windows: `.claude.json` is saved as a whole-file read-modify-write behind a best-effort lock that is abandoned after 15 s — entries written by one process disappear again, trust flags included. Facts from the issue body only.

      Every change to ~/.claude.json is a whole-file read-modify-write. The only cross-process protection is an advisory lock created with mkdir. It is given up in three ways: after a 15 s wait the writer proceeds without it, a lock older than 10 s may be deleted and taken over, and if mkdir fails outright the writer proceeds without it too. Nothing surfaces above warn. On the reporter machine the lock was abandoned or stolen 8 times between 2026-08-25 and 2026-09-08. hasTrustDialogAccepted keys were written and are gone again.

      Environment: Windows 11 Enterprise 10.0.26200; Claude Desktop 1.46388.4, unchanged in 1.49585.0 (installed 2026-09-08 18:42); host CLI 2.1.260; many parallel sessions across worktrees; ~/.claude.json 215.7 KB / 334 project keys. Constants: Bvn/QSn = 1e4 (10 s steal), Vvn/$Sn = 15e3 (15 s timeout). Newest timeout one hour after 1.49585.0 install. A hand-added key with the app closed did not survive seven minutes after reopen. Diagnostic assay only — no live Claude, no secrets, no network to Anthropic.
