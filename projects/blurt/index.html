Research brief

        

          Primary OPEN issue:
          anthropics/claude-code#92275.
          Title: Terminal identification queries are sent during a ~50ms window where ECHO is re-enabled, leaking replies to the screen at startup.
          Labels: bug, has repro, platform:linux, area:tui.
        

        

          Cousin cite-only:
          #91530
          (claude --resume under tmux — probe replies read as input)
          and
          #87459
          (Windows Terminal PowerShell: raw ANSI mouse tracking sequences echoed to screen). Different symptom. Cite only.
        

        

          Backups (document only, not built):
          #92286 Cresset,
          #92292 Symlink,
          #92269 Louver.
        

      


      

        
1 — Cooked ECHO after teardown

        

          On startup Claude Code briefly restores the tty to cooked mode
          (ECHO on). Timestamped pty evidence: ECHO on→off correctly at ~0.040;
          later at ~0.202 ECHO restored ON after focus/bracketed-paste teardown.
        

        
2 — Identification queries inside the window

        

          Inside that window the client emits CSI > 0 q (XTVERSION)
          and CSI c (Primary DA). At ~0.206 those queries leave
          with ECHO still on. That is the bug.
        

        
3 — VTE answers inside the cooked window

        

          xfce4-terminal 1.2.0 / VTE 0.84.1 replies in ~0.1–14ms median —
          well inside the ~47–51ms cooked window. The kernel line discipline
          echoes those replies in caret notation above the banner:
          ^[[I, ^[P>|VTE(...), …1;22;28c.
        

        
4 — The later pulse works

        

          At ~0.253 ECHO is off again. At ~0.317 the same queries are sent
          correctly with ECHO off. The probe itself works — only the first
          premature emission leaks.
        

        
Expected (from the issue — checklist, not implemented here)

        

          Never emit identification queries while ECHO is on; keep raw mode
          through the probe; no caret-notation leak above the banner.
        

        
Likely path (NON-BINDING)

        

          The desk should make “XTVERSION + Primary DA emitted inside a cooked
          ECHO window after focus/bracketed-paste teardown” visceral. Discard if
          evidence disagrees. Do not claim unseen source.
